# Vision

Opporlligence aims to become a persistent opportunity-intelligence system that observes changes in the real world and reasons about their consequences.

The long-term system should be able to:

1. Observe developments across multiple information domains.
2. Establish what the world looked like before a development.
3. Determine what materially changed.
4. Connect the development to other historical and current developments.
5. Identify newly possible actions, newly created constraints, and unresolved gaps.
6. Generate candidate opportunities without treating the user's current skills as a hard boundary.
7. Search for existing alternatives and counter-evidence.
8. Estimate confidence, significance, urgency, and uncertainty.
9. Remember opportunities and track how they evolve over time.
10. Alert only when a development or opportunity crosses a meaningful threshold.

The desired outcome is not an endless stream of startup ideas. It is a system that helps answer:

> **What is becoming possible, what is becoming difficult, what is missing, and what deserves investigation?**

## Non-goals

Opporlligence is not intended to be:

- a conventional news aggregator;
- a generic AI idea generator;
- a personalized echo chamber;
- a system that assumes every change creates a business;
- a hard filter based on the user's existing abilities.

## Ultimate standard

An opportunity should survive contact with evidence, existing alternatives, contradictions, and time.
